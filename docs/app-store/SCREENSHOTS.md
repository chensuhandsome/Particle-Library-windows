# App Store screenshot checklist

## Required display sizes

Capture at native resolutions, not upscaled.

| Device slot | Size (portrait) | Required |
|---|---|---|
| iPhone 6.9-inch | 1320 × 2868 or 1290 × 2796 | yes |
| iPhone 6.5-inch | 1284 × 2778 or 1242 × 2688 | yes (if no 6.9 upload) |
| iPad 13-inch | 2064 × 2752 or 2048 × 2732 | yes if iPad is supported |

Add 6.7-inch/6.1-inch only if you want device-specific layouts.

## Shot list (English and Chinese)

1. **Search / home** — search field, recent queries and PDG 2026 marker.
2. **Particle page** — χc1(3872) identity, quantum numbers, mass/width and
   context sidebar.
3. **Measurement page** — pion polarizability or a decay measurement with
   errors and references.
4. **Notes** — a note with a PDG anchor, tags and a collection.
5. **PDF review** — a highlight with the two range handles visible and the
   annotation sidebar open.
6. **Full catalog** — the catalog entry from the top bar/overflow menu.
7. **Settings → About and coverage** — version and update state.

## Presentation rules

- Use light and dark mode consistently; do not mix modes in one set.
- No device frames required; keep one visual style.
- Do not show placeholder/loading states, debug banners or a mouse cursor.
- Do not claim official PDG affiliation.
- The first screenshot should communicate “offline PDG reader”.
- Verify all text is readable at thumbnail size.
- Localize captions in App Store Connect for English and Chinese.
